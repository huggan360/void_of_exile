# Tavern grass, options and shield update

Tavern ground decoration previously shared island-wide MultiMeshes with a 60 m visibility cutoff at the island origin. Nearby grass vanished when the player walked away from the center. Decorations now use 32 m spatial batches with local bounds and ordinary frustum culling. Placement and density remain authored/deterministic. The Tavern itself remains the fixed editable scene.

Options is available from both the title screen and Escape menu. General, Audio and Graphics are intentionally empty. Video contains windowed/fullscreen mode, resolution, display selection, V-Sync, FPS cap, resizable window, FPS readout and GUI size. Fullscreen resolution changes the 3D render resolution while preserving native GUI sharpness; windowed resolution changes the actual window. Applying video previews for 15 seconds; Keep saves, Revert/Escape/timeout restores. Preferences live separately from characters and realms in `user://settings.cfg`.

Small / Medium / Large GUI multipliers are 0.85 / 1 / 1.2. UI scales proportionally with the window against the original desktop dimensions. Medium preserves the old fullscreen sizes. HUD, menus, loading transitions, inventory tooltips and interaction prompts share the scale; camera aiming remains in native viewport coordinates.

Controls exposes 19 actions under Movement, Combat, Camera and Interface. Each row shows current/default bindings and Reset. Clicking opens a key/mouse capture modal; Escape cancels. Conflicts require an explicit swap and bindings save automatically. Reset All restores defaults. Existing developer keys F3/F4/F6/F7/F9 remain reserved. HUD hints, quickslot labels, minimap controls and NPC interaction prompts follow the selected mapping.

All Brown/Blue/Purple Spirit ruin chances and wilderness densities are now **30% of their previous values**, with no redistribution into replacement spirit packs. Standard ruins: Brown 13.5%, Blue 3.6%, Purple 1.5%, empty 81.4%. Bluelay ruins: Brown 13.5%, Blue 6%, Purple 1.5%, empty 79%. Other gods retain their rates. Finite-death records are preserved. Current seed 20261031 has 241 planned/streamed pack sites; refreshed full rates are in `BIOME_ENEMY_SPAWNS.md` and the texture-folder TXT copy.

Shielding reduces movement to **15% of normal stat-scaled speed** and prevents firing. Raising the shield resolves before movement/weapons in the same frame and cancels unfinished charge, windup and burst attacks. Enemy projectiles collide with the visible shield surface using swept collision. Every contact flashes and directionally deforms the bubble, with colored debris sampled from the actual projectile mesh. Magic is blocked completely; physical hits retain their existing partial mitigation and shield-capacity cost. Shield contact consumes piercing/returning shots and suppresses their secondary rings/terminal volleys.

The Ruin Watcher's rune orbs are magic. Player magic rings now interfere with enemy magic projectiles: the contacted ring arc and projectile spend power against one another. A stronger ring destroys the projectile; a stronger projectile continues at reduced damage; near-equal powers cancel. Each wave affects a shot once. Ordinary physical projectiles pass through rings.
