# Boss intros, fortress gates and the expanded realm

Realm radius is now 640 blocks (previously 400). Authored towns remain at exactly the same coordinates: Hearthwick (0,0), Bramblebrook (88,-47), Snowbell (198,7), Sunveil (-170,115). Strongholds moved outward: Cinder (172,-475), Rime (505,17), Azure (140,485), Sun (-406,275), Hollow (-399,-310). The forest/town belt keeps its previous extent. Frost mountains are broader; the inward gate approach caps the fortress floor height to keep it walkable. Three approach roads extend outward; the static layout and editor reference map were refreshed.

Boss introductions default to 10 seconds. Every admitted living player inside the fortress gets a slow orbiting camera, letterbox bars and the boss name. Movement/weapon actions lock independently of menu locks; the server rejects attempted actions/movement and protects participants during the intro with a short combat handoff grace. Travel, reset, death and disconnect restore normal presentation. Admission is specific to each player; distant and late-entering players are not pulled into someone else's intro.

Vharok, Scarab Regent and Hollow Crown close both entrances, starting in the intro and reopening on reset/death/removal. Blue Dragon and Rimeheart remain open. A gate waits for players to clear its opening before closing. Dynamic gate bodies avoid mutating terrain plans read by voxel workers. The same closure reaches every client reliably, is included on realm entry and is enforced against reported server movement. `lock_gates` and `intro_time` are configurable in server boss settings.

Entering within the fortress walls reveals the whole 105×105 fortress footprint plus an eight-block margin in fog of war, immediately and for the remainder of that visit. This includes arena, walls, entrances and apron, and updates the minimap's exploration texture. Other wilderness remains hidden.

Version-5 realm saves migrate to layout version 6 without wiping casualties or destroyed objects. Boss and guard pack progress is copied to their moved fortress IDs. Older migrations retain their preexisting archive behavior. Network protocol is now 2/version 0.9.1, so restart/update server and clients together. Enemy/player snapshot components are sent separately to avoid combining otherwise-small slices into oversized UDP packets.

Validation completed before the subsequent request to stop testing: 111 focused intro/gate/migration checks, 55,142 fortress-fog checks, 57,642 fortress geometry checks, 171,837 boss encounter checks, and a real dedicated server plus three ENet clients using an isolated local account service (server 8 checks, clients 12/11/4 checks), all passing. Two players exercised hatch, camera, control lock, gate closure, health scaling, reset, retry and death. A third joined with gates closed, received the current state, stayed out of the cinematic and observed reopen events. The later per-viewer roster/snapshot packet separation is implemented but was not rerun after the user asked for scripts only/no testing.
