# Legendary weapon identity review

Included in 26.5-beta after visual review. The canonical
weapon-specific icon grids, primary formations, charged formations and plain
language descriptions live in `items/signatures.json`.

All 29 Legendary weapons have individual projectile silhouettes and motion
profiles in `LegendaryProjectiles`. Primary volleys sum to 1.0 damage share;
charged volleys sum to the existing charge multiplier. Ash keeps its established
inferno/ring secondary. Base damage, item bonuses and drop chances are unchanged.
Some volleys have different projectile counts, speed ratios and short launch
intervals to make the weapon feel different, rather than just recoloring it.
Delayed launches are replicated to remote viewers and stay bounded to 0.5 s.

The 11 bow icons use 18-23 filled pixels, compared with 22 on G1/G4, retaining
diagonal strings and open interiors. Every Legendary weapon has a distinct
8x8 occupancy mask. PNGs and editable Pixelorama sources match.

Review artifacts are generated by running `tools/preview_legendary_review.tscn`
with the graphical renderer, then `python3 -B tools/make_legendary_review.py`.
The actual in-game meshes and sampled `Arrows._position_at` trajectories become
one full PNG sheet plus Bow, Wand and Trident close-ups. The plots are an oblique
view of sampled flight paths; they are not screenshots of particle trails.
Timing and the existing Ash impact ring are described in the text, not animated.

The tests `tools/test_legendary_identities.tscn` and
`tools/test_signature_items.tscn` exercise the real firing paths, geometry,
trajectory uniqueness, finite planet-relative directions, shared damage budgets,
launch delays, particles, Artifact bursts and worn appearances.

The signature catalog reflects the new projectile definitions. Version 26.5-beta
uses protocol 13 so clients and servers run matching firing patterns.
